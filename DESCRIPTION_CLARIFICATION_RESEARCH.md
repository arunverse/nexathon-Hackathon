# Description Clarification Research

This document outlines the common vagueness patterns observed in business and product descriptions across different industry families. It serves as the design specification for the AI-driven clarification engine.

## 1. SaaS / Software
*   **Vague Examples:** "We are a SaaS building company", "Software platform for businesses", "B2B SaaS tool"
*   **Missing Info:** Exact category/niche, target audience size, buyer persona.
*   **Clarifying Questions:**
    *   *Question:* What category of software do you provide?
        *   *Options:* HR Tech, FinTech Infrastructure, HealthTech, Logistics SaaS, Vertical SaaS, Marketing Tech, DevTools, CRM/Sales, Other.
    *   *Question:* Who typically buys your software?
        *   *Options:* Enterprise (Sales-led), Mid-market, SMBs (Self-serve), B2B2C, Consumers.

## 2. IT Services / Consulting
*   **Vague Examples:** "We do IT services", "Tech consulting firm", "Software development agency"
*   **Missing Info:** Specific service lines, project types, typical client size/industry.
*   **Clarifying Questions:**
    *   *Question:* What are your primary service offerings?
        *   *Options:* Web Development, Mobile App Dev, Cloud/DevOps, ERP Implementation, Cybersecurity, Data/BI, Staff Augmentation, UI/UX Design, Other.
    *   *Question:* How do you typically engage with clients?
        *   *Options:* Project-based builds, Long-term retainers / maintenance, Dedicated teams.

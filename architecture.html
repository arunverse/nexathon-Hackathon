1. High-Level System Architecture

EBEXTA operates on a multi-tier architecture, heavily leveraging edge computing, serverless AI orchestration, and real-time database synchronization. The core loop consists of the Frontend Client (React/Vite), the Backend Data Layer (Supabase), and the Autonomous AI Engine (Gemini / Serper).

2. The Autonomous Scan Workflow

The heart of EBEXTA is the Scan Engine. It doesn't just search; it actively crawls, reads, and reasons about companies to ensure they perfectly match the user's Buyer Profile. The process uses a multi-stage validation pipeline to guarantee zero hallucinations.

3. Core Data Entities & Memory Architecture

Data in EBEXTA is separated into ephemeral/search-bound data (`leads`) and permanent, ever-growing corporate intelligence (`company_memory`).

4. Deep Intelligence & Dynamic Rendering

When a user interacts with a company, EBEXTA generates deep intelligence on the fly. Instead of burning tokens during the initial bulk scan, the Deal Strategy, Business Impact, and Relationship Maps are generated just-in-time when the Company Dossier is opened, and cached forever in Supabase.

Just-In-Time Generation

Heavy AI tasks like drafting Neural Emails, calculating precise Deal Sizes, and plotting Stakeholder maps are deferred until explicit user request to optimize speed and cost.

Fallback Resilience

If a lead fails to insert into the primary table due to race conditions or payload limits, the UI intelligently falls back to reconstructing the lead from the `company_memory` snapshot, ensuring zero data loss for the user.

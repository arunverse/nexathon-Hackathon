# EBEXTA Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 July 2026
**Last updated:** 23 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Ebexta, operating the "EBEXTA" platform** ("we", "us")
collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data in connection with the EBEXTA website,
application, and any related features or services (the "**Service**"). It applies to everyone
who uses the Service and, where relevant, to individuals whose business contact information
appears in Leads. Written to be **feature-agnostic** "the Service" includes any feature we
add, change, or remove.

By using the Service you acknowledge this Policy.

---

## 1. Who we are
**Ebexta** operates the EBEXTA platform and is the data fiduciary/controller for personal
data processed via the Service. Your primary privacy contact is **your EBEXTA representative**
— the team member who contacted you after you submitted your plan request (see §12).

## 2. Categories of data we process

**(a) Account & identity data** name, work email, password (stored **hashed** by our
auth provider; we never see it), and, for Google sign-in, the profile info Google shares.

**(b) Business/profile data you provide** company name, website, mission/offering, mode
(seeker/provider), role, phone, industry, and any company-profile details you enter.

**(c) Usage & technical data** actions in the app, scans run, credits used, log/event
data, approximate technical metadata from your requests, and data stored locally in your
browser (see §7).

**(d) Content you create** chat messages, attachments, saved leads, outreach drafts,
plan/dossier requests.

**(e) Third-party business-contact data ("Leads")** company and business decision-maker
information (e.g. name, role, business email/phone, LinkedIn, public evidence) that the
Service surfaces from **public and third-party sources** via automated search and AI. This
may include personal data of individuals who are not our users.

We do **not** intentionally collect sensitive personal data (e.g. financial account numbers,
health, biometrics) and ask you not to submit it.

## 3. How we collect data
- **Directly from you** (signup, onboarding, settings, forms, chat).
- **Automatically** (usage/log data, local storage).
- **From third parties / public sources** for Leads, via automated web search and AI
  grounding over publicly available information and licensed/third-party sources.

## 4. Why we process data (purposes & legal basis)
| Purpose | Basis (illustrative) |
|---|---|
| Provide, operate, and secure the Service | Contract / legitimate interest |
| Generate and verify Leads | Contract / legitimate interest |
| Bill, manage plans and Credits | Contract |
| Support, communicate service notices | Contract / legitimate interest |
| Improve and develop the Service | Legitimate interest |
| Comply with law, prevent abuse/fraud | Legal obligation / legitimate interest |
| Marketing (only if you opt in) | Consent |

For Leads, the legal basis is our and our customers' **legitimate interest in B2B business
development**, processing publicly available business-contact data subject to the rights
in §9.

## 5. AI processing & third-party sub-processors
To generate Leads we send **business search context (e.g. your mission and target company
names) to an AI/search provider (Google Gemini with Google Search grounding).** We do **not**
send your account password or unrelated personal data to the AI provider. Our key
sub-processors:
- **Supabase** database, authentication, storage, serverless functions.
- **Google (Gemini / Search)** AI generation and grounding.
- **Vercel** website/app hosting.
- **Google OAuth** optional social sign-in.
These providers process data under their own terms and may store data outside India (see §8).

## 6. How we share data
We do **not sell** personal data and do **not** show third-party advertising. We share data
only: (a) with the sub-processors above to run the Service; (b) between connected users
where you choose to interact (e.g. chat, and the safe profile fields shown to a chat
partner); (c) to comply with law, court orders, or to protect rights and safety; (d) in a
business transfer (merger/acquisition), with notice.

## 7. Cookies & local storage
The Service uses essential browser **local storage** to keep you signed in and to cache your
leads, filters, and drafts on your device. We do not use third-party advertising/tracking
cookies. Clearing your browser storage or signing out removes this local data (we wipe our
keys on sign-out).

## 8. International transfers
Our providers may process/store data in regions outside India (e.g. the US/EU). Where
required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards and the providers' compliance programs.

## 9. Your rights
Subject to applicable law (DPDP Act / GDPR), you may request to **access, correct, update,
or delete** your personal data, **withdraw consent**, or **object to** certain processing.
- **Users:** you can edit your profile in-app and **delete your account** (Settings → Danger
  Zone), which removes your data from our systems and revokes your login.
- **Individuals appearing in Leads:** you may request access, correction, or removal of your
  business-contact data from our systems by contacting us (§12); we will action verified
  requests as required by law.
To exercise rights, contact your EBEXTA representative (§12). We may verify your identity
before acting.

## 10. Data retention
We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
Account data is retained while your account is active and deleted (or anonymised) on account
deletion, subject to legal retention and backup cycles. Cached Leads and logs are retained
for limited operational periods and then expire or are purged.

## 11. Security
We use industry practices: encryption in transit (TLS), hashed passwords (bcrypt via our
auth provider), row-level access controls so users can only access their own data,
least-privilege server secrets, and restricted file storage. **No system is 100% secure;**
we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the Service at your own risk.

## 12. Privacy contact, grievances & cooperation
For any privacy question, request, or complaint, **contact your EBEXTA representative** the
team member who reached out to you after you submitted your plan request. They are your first
point of contact and will handle or escalate your request.
**If your representative refers you to another EBEXTA team member, our designated grievance
officer, or a data-protection specialist** to resolve your matter, **please cooperate with
that person and provide the information reasonably requested until the matter is fully
resolved.** We aim to acknowledge requests promptly and resolve them within the period
required by law.

## 13. Children
The Service is for business users **18+**. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 14. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Policy as the Service evolves; the "Last updated" date will change and
material changes will be notified in-app or by email. Continued use means acceptance.

Operated by **Ebexta**.

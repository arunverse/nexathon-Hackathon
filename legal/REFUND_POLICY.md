# EBEXTA Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** 23 July 2026
**Last updated:** 23 July 2026

This Policy explains refunds and cancellations for paid plans and Credits on EBEXTA
("**Service**", operated by **Ebexta**). It forms part of the Terms & Conditions. It is
written to apply to **any current or future paid feature or Credit type**, so it survives
changes to the product.

---

## 1. What you're buying
EBEXTA is a **digital SaaS service**. Paid access is delivered as **plans** and/or
**Credits** that are **consumed** when you use paid features (e.g. running a scan, generating
Leads, AI outputs). Credits and access are **licences to use the Service, not physical
goods.**

## 2. General rule (digital, consumption-based)
Because the Service delivers digital value that is **consumed on use** and incurs real
third-party costs the moment you use it:
- **Consumed Credits and used services are non-refundable.**
- **Active subscription/plan fees are non-refundable** for time already elapsed.
This is standard for consumption-based SaaS. Exceptions are below.

## 3. When we **will** consider a refund
We act in good faith. You may be eligible for a full or partial refund if:
- **(a) Service failure on our side** you paid, but a verified technical fault on our end
  prevented you from using the Credits/features you bought, and we could not fix it within a
  reasonable time.
- **(b) Duplicate or erroneous charge** you were charged twice or in error.
- **(c) Unactivated purchase** you paid but access/Credits were never activated and you no
  longer want them.
- **(d) Cooling-off** a refund of **unused** Credits requested within **7 days** of
  purchase, provided **no Credits have been consumed** and no Leads were delivered.

## 4. When we **will not** refund
- Credits already consumed, or scans/Leads already delivered.
- Dissatisfaction with **Lead outcomes** because Leads are AI-generated/sourced and we
  **do not guarantee results, conversions, or accuracy** (see the Terms, §4–§5). Not liking
  the results is not a service failure.
- Change of mind after Credits were used.
- Accounts suspended or terminated for **breach or abuse** of the Terms.
- Third-party/network issues outside our control.
- Free trials, promotional, bonus, or complimentary Credits.
- Amounts beyond the liability cap in the Terms.

## 5. Plan changes, expiry & cancellation
- **Cancellation:** you may cancel/stop using the Service at any time. Cancellation stops
  future activation/renewal; it does **not** refund elapsed time or used Credits.
- **Expiry:** Credits and plan validity expire as stated at purchase and **do not roll over**
  unless we say so. Expired Credits are not refundable.
- **Downgrades/upgrades:** take effect as described at the time; differences are handled as
  Credits or adjustments, not cash refunds, unless required by law.

## 6. How to request a refund
**Contact your EBEXTA representative** the team member who reached out to you after you
submitted your plan request within **7 days** of the charge, with: your account email, the
transaction/reference, the amount, and the reason. We may ask for details to verify the issue.
**If your representative refers you to another EBEXTA team member or a billing specialist** to
resolve your request, **please cooperate with that person until the matter is fully resolved.**

## 7. Processing
Approved refunds are made to the **original payment method** (or as agreed) within **7–14
business days** of approval. Timing to reflect in your account depends on your bank or payment
provider. Refunds are net of any non-refundable third-party fees where applicable.

## 8. Chargebacks
Please contact your EBEXTA representative first most issues are resolved quickly. Initiating
a chargeback without contacting us may lead to **suspension of your account** pending
resolution. Fraudulent chargebacks may be pursued.

## 9. Currency & taxes
Refunds are issued in the **original currency (₹ INR unless stated)**. Taxes (e.g. GST) are
refunded only to the extent legally recoverable.

## 10. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Policy as the Service evolves; the version in effect at the time of your
purchase applies to that purchase. Continued use after changes means acceptance.

Operated by **Ebexta**.

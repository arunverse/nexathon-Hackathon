# DPDP & Data Handling

**Effective Date:** 10 October 2026
**Last Updated:** 10 October 2026

---

## 1. Who Uses EBEXTA

EBEXTA is an enterprise Business-to-Business (B2B) Business Intelligence and Business Network platform built for commercial entities, companies, and business organisations.

- **Enterprise Focus:** Accounts on EBEXTA are registered by and represent commercial business entities, not individual consumers.
- **Authorised Representatives:** Individuals using EBEXTA act solely as Authorised Representatives of their respective companies.
- **DPDP Alignment:** EBEXTA is designed to operate in alignment with the principles of India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and international commercial data protection standards.

This document provides a concise, plain-language summary of how we process, handle, and protect data across our platform.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

We process limited categories of data necessary to provide our B2B services:

- **Customer & Representative Data:** Business email address, full name, job title, company name, corporate domain, and login authentication records.
- **Commercial & Billing Records:** Selected subscription plans, Credit allocations, and transaction reference numbers.
- **Network Interactions:** Connection requests, direct B2B messages, and profile information published on the EBEXTA B2B Network.
- **System Telemetry:** IP addresses, technical logs, device indicators, and audit timestamps collected for security monitoring and fraud prevention.

---

## 3. Business Intelligence Data

EBEXTA collects, structures, and analyses business information to assist companies in discovering market opportunities and evaluating prospective commercial counterparties.

- **Data Sources:** EBEXTA collects and analyses publicly available and permitted third-party business information using automated search, data processing, AI-assisted analysis, and internal intelligence algorithms.
- **Nature of Information:** Intelligence records comprise professional details such as corporate names, official business domains, corporate roles, executive affiliations, industry classifications, public business signals, and corporate contact references.
- **Professional Capacity:** Information relates strictly to individuals in their professional or corporate capacities, not as private consumers.
- **Accuracy Reality:** Business data changes continuously across industries. Information surfaced through automated indexing may be incomplete, outdated, or inferred. Users must independently verify critical intelligence prior to entering into contractual or commercial commitments.
- **No Blanket Manual Verification:** We do not claim that every contact or data point is manually validated by human personnel. Automated search, sector algorithms, and web grounding form the core of our indexing system.
- **No Sale of Consumer Personal Data:** EBEXTA does not sell consumer personal data, does not trade in private residential profiles, and does not operate behavioural ad networks.

---

## 4. Why We Process Data (Purpose Limitation)

Under the DPDP framework, data must be processed only for specified, lawful purposes. EBEXTA processes data strictly to:

- Deliver the core B2B intelligence flow: **DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → ENGAGE → CONNECT**.
- Power the EBEXTA B2B Network, enabling commercial organisations to connect and communicate.
- Authenticate authorised business representatives and enforce secure access controls.
- Administer billing, track usage Credits, and fulfill statutory tax obligations.
- Protect infrastructure against malicious scraping, automated abuse, and cybersecurity threats.

We do not repurpose data for unrelated commercial activities without appropriate lawful basis.

## 5. AI-Assisted Processing

- **Advanced AI Integration:** EBEXTA incorporates artificial intelligence models and search grounding technologies (including Google Gemini foundational models and web search grounding) to synthesize company context, analyze commercial intent, and generate executive summaries.
- **Analytical & Generative Outputs:** AI-generated outputs, dossiers, and intent scores are assistive analytical tools. They represent algorithmic synthesis and probabilistic inferences rather than guaranteed factual determinations.
- **Independent Assessment:** Customers are expected to apply professional judgment and independently evaluate AI-generated outputs before making business decisions.

## 6. B2B Network Data

The EBEXTA B2B Network enables verified businesses and decision-makers to discover each other:

- **Business Visibility:** Company details, executive titles, and business capabilities published by a Customer are visible to other verified participants on the network.
- **Communication Controls:** Users can manage connection requests and control inbound messaging preferences.
- **Anti-Spam Standards:** Network participants are prohibited from engaging in unsolicited bulk broadcasting, commercial spam, or deceptive outreach.

---

## 7. Security and Access Controls

We employ multi-layered technical and organizational security controls to protect data:

- **Encryption:** All web traffic is encrypted in transit via modern TLS/HTTPS protocols; databases and backups are encrypted at rest with industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
- **Tenant Isolation:** Access to account records is enforced at the database level through strict owner-scoped access rules.
- **Authentication Protections:** Passwords are cryptographically hashed; multi-factor authentication and single sign-on (SSO) options are supported.
- **Audit Logging:** Administrative operations, security events, and authentication attempts are logged for security oversight.

---

## 8. Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** Data associated with active business accounts is retained for the duration of the commercial relationship.
- **Account Closure:** When a Customer closes its account or submits a verified deletion request, associated personal account data is purged or anonymized within thirty (30) days, except where retention is legally mandated for statutory accounting, tax, or regulatory compliance.
- **Intelligence Index:** Business intelligence records are periodically refreshed, updated, or suppressed in accordance with our data maintenance schedules.

---

## 9. Data Principal Rights

In alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Data Principals possess fundamental rights regarding their personal data:

- **Right to Access:** Request confirmation of processing and a summary of personal data held.
- **Right to Correction & Updating:** Request correction or completion of inaccurate, outdated, or misleading personal data.
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal retention obligations.
- **Right to Grievance Redressal:** Access readily available mechanisms to address concerns or questions regarding data handling.
- **Right to Nominate:** Nominate an individual to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity, as prescribed under the DPDP Act and its implementing rules.

Business professionals whose public contact details appear in our intelligence results may request review or suppression from search results through our designated grievance channel.

## 3. Business Customer and Authorised Representative

To understand our data processing, it is important to distinguish between the entities involved:

- **Customer:** The legal business, company, partnership, or enterprise that contracts with EBEXTA and owns the EBEXTA account.
- **Authorised Representative / User:** The individual professional who registers, accesses, or operates the account on Customer's behalf (e.g., founder, executive, sales director, or procurement specialist).

## 4. Categories of Information We Process

We process the following categories of information:

- **Account & Identity Data:** Full name, business email address, job title, company name, corporate domain, hashed passwords, and authentication tokens.
- **Corporate & Profile Data:** Industry sector, company size, office location, service capabilities, procurement requirements, and public website address.
- **Commercial & Billing Data:** Subscription plan selection, credit balance, transaction timestamps, invoice history, and masked payment details (full card numbers are processed directly by our PCI-DSS certified payment processors).
- **Communication & Network Data:** Connection requests, direct business messages, participation in sector forums, profile interactions, and notes recorded on the platform.
- **Technical & Telemetry Data:** Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, device operating system, referring URL, time stamps, user agent, and audit log entries.
- **B2B Business Intelligence Data:** Publicly available and commercial data regarding companies, market signals, executive roles, business contact details, and evidence citations.

## 17. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical and organizational measures designed to protect data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, or alteration:

- **Encryption in Transit:** All communications with the Service are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
- **Encryption at Rest:** Databases, backups, and storage volumes are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
- **Tenant Isolation:** Access to customer records is governed by strict owner-scoped database policies.
- **Credential Protection:** Passwords and sensitive authentication tokens are hashed using cryptographic one-way functions; plaintext credentials are never accessible to our staff.
- **Vulnerability Monitoring:** Ongoing code reviews, automated dependency audits, and continuous security monitoring.

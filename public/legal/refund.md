**Effective Date:** 10 October 2026

## 1. Scope

This Refund & Cancellation Policy ("**Policy**") governs all commercial purchases made on or in connection with the EBEXTA platform, including subscription plans, top-up usage Credits, custom enterprise packages, and paid digital services (collectively, the "**Service**").

This Policy forms an integral part of the EBEXTA Terms & Conditions and applies to all business customers, companies, and commercial accounts.

---

## 2. Digital B2B Service

EBEXTA is an enterprise digital software-as-a-service (SaaS) and business intelligence platform.

- Access to paid subscription features, intelligence capabilities, AI-assisted tools, and the EBEXTA B2B Network is activated immediately upon successful transaction processing.
- Usage Credits are digital units provisioned immediately to Customer's account and consumed dynamically as intelligence queries, deep scans, data enrichments, or network interactions are initiated.
- Because digital access and data indexing capabilities are delivered immediately upon purchase, all transactions are treated as final.

## 3. No-Refund Policy

EBEXTA operates under a strict **no-refund** policy across all paid products and services:

> **All purchases, subscriptions, plans, Credits and paid digital services are non-refundable except where a refund is required by applicable law or expressly agreed by EBEXTA in writing.**

Without limiting the generality of the foregoing:

- **No Refunds for Used Credits:** Credits that have been expended or consumed for any query, scan, or service action are strictly non-refundable.
- **No Refunds for Unused Credits:** Unused, accumulated, or remaining Credits are non-refundable and hold no monetary cash value.
- **No Outcome-Based Refunds:** EBEXTA provides analytical tools, data intelligence, and business networking access; we do **not** guarantee commercial transactions. The following circumstances do **not** qualify for a refund or credit under any circumstance:
  - Dissatisfaction with search results, company dossiers, or AI-generated intelligence.
  - Accuracy, completeness, or freshness variations in specific company data or contact records.
  - Lack of response, rejection, or non-engagement from a discovered counterparty or executive.
  - Failure to schedule meetings, secure contracts, close sales, or generate business revenue.
  - Customer's internal decision to alter commercial priorities, sales strategies, or budget allocations.
  - Inactivity or failure to utilize an active subscription during the paid billing cycle.

## 4. Plans and Subscriptions

- **Advance Billing:** Subscriptions are billed in advance on a recurring periodic basis (e.g., monthly or annually) according to the plan selected by Customer.
- **Mid-Cycle Cancellation:** Customers may cancel recurring subscriptions at any time before the next billing cycle. When a subscription is cancelled mid-cycle, access remains active until the end of the current prepaid billing period.
- **No Pro-Rata Refunds:** EBEXTA does not issue pro-rata refunds, cash reimbursements, or partial credits for unused days or time remaining in a billing cycle.

---

## 5. Credits

- **Nature of Credits:** Credits represent digital access allocations specifically designed to measure platform usage.
- **No Monetary Value:** Credits do not constitute a deposit, electronic money, stored-value instrument, or property. Credits have no cash surrender value and cannot be redeemed, returned, refunded, or exchanged for fiat currency or cash equivalents.
- **Promotional & Bonus Credits:** Any complimentary, trial, promotional, or bonus credits granted by EBEXTA carry zero monetary value, are strictly non-transferable, and expire in accordance with the specific terms of the promotion.
- **Non-Transferability:** Credits cannot be transferred, assigned, or gifted across separate business accounts unless authorized in writing under an enterprise corporate contract.

---

## 6. Cancellation and Non-Renewal

- **How to Cancel:** Customer's Authorised Representative may cancel automatic subscription renewal directly via the account settings panel or by providing written non-renewal notice to their assigned EBEXTA representative prior to the renewal date.
- **Effective Date of Non-Renewal:** Cancellation takes effect at the expiration of the current paid billing cycle. Customer will not be billed for subsequent cycles.
- **Post-Cancellation Access:** Following the end of the paid billing cycle, the account reverts to a restricted or inactive state. Historical saved searches and stored dossiers may be archived or removed in accordance with our data retention schedule.

## 7. Account Suspension / Termination for Cause

- A material breach of the Terms & Conditions or Acceptable Use Policy;
- System abuse, scraping, automated data harvesting, or reverse engineering;
- Unlawful commercial solicitation, harassment, spam, or fraud; or
- Any activity threatening the security, stability, or integrity of the Service or B2B Network,

Customer immediately forfeits all remaining subscription time, features, and Credit balances. **No refund, reimbursement, or compensation will be provided for terminated accounts.**
